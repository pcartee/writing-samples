## Subscription levels

Trust Authority provides several subscription levels: Enterprise, and Starter. Each level has different service limits and quotas, as follows.

| Plan          	| Max attestation API keys (workloads)  | Max management API keys	| Max tenant admins	| Max tenant users	| Policies  |
|-------------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|-----------|	
| Starter                              | 1	                       | 2	                        | 2	                | 100	            |  10           |
| Enterprise – Base Config	           | 5	                       | 2	                        | 5	                | 100	            |  10           |     
| Enterprise one additional workload   | 6	                       | 2	                        | 5	                | 100	            |  15           |
| Enterprise three additional workloads | 8	                       | 2	                        | 5	                | 100	            |  20           | 
| Enterprise five additional workloads  | 10	                       | 2	                        | 5	                | 100	            |  25           |
| Enterprise - Custom	               | 100	                   | 2	                        | 5	                | 100	            |  50           |

### Changing your subscription level    

You can change or cancel your subscription at any time. [Contact your sales representative](mailto:trustauthority@intel.com) to make any changes.

## Registering and logging on using 3rd party identity systems

When a new user is registered with Trust Authority, an invitation email containing a link is sent to the user. The user must perform the steps below to complete the registration:

1. Click the link in the email. 
1. Enter your email address and authenticate with your credentials. If your email address is not associated with a 3rd party identity system, a code is sent to your email address. Use the code to log in. 
1. Accept the guest consent agreement.
1. Register multi-factor authentication. Use a time-based authenticator application such as the Microsoft Authenticator* or the Google Authenticator*, or a one-time passcode via text or voice message on your phone.
1. Please read and accept the Terms of Use.
1. At the Trust Authority Portal, click **Continue** to acknowledge that you have read the Community Terms and Service Agreement.

#### Returning users

Users returning to the Trust Authority portal will use the following steps:

1. Access the Client Portal.
1. Enter your email address and password.
1. Complete authentication using your authenticator application or the one-time passcode via text or voice message on your phone.
